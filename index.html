Guest Notes

    

      
Where can I park?
Parking is available at St. Joseph’s Church Mandapam, Vattakarai (Wedding & Lunch) and at St. Antony’s Church Mandapam, Azhagappapuram (Reception). Please arrive a little early.

      
Are children welcome?
Yes, please bring the whole family. Everyone is welcome.

      
When is the lunch?
Lunch is served at St. Joseph’s Church Mandapam right after the Mass.

      
How do I get there?
By train: Nagercoil Junction is the nearest station, a short auto or taxi ride from Vattakarai.
By bus: Take any bus to Nagercoil, then an auto to the venue.
By air: Trivandrum International Airport is about 75 km away, roughly 2 hours by road. Taxis are available from the airport.

      
Where can I stay?
Nagercoil has many hotels and lodges. Message us on WhatsApp and we will gladly help.
